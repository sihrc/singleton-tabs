# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for
everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while discussing the work on its merits.
- Welcome questions and help contributors participate effectively.
- Accept feedback gracefully and acknowledge mistakes.
- Protect other people's privacy and remove sensitive information from examples.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's private
information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report abusive content
through GitHub's reporting tools. Reports will be considered in good faith and
handled with attention to the safety and privacy of everyone involved.

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and
other spaces representing the project.
